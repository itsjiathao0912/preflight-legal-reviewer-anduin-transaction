# Section 5. Investor Representations and Warranties

The Investor represents, warrants and agrees, as of the date it signs this Agreement and as of each Closing at which it is admitted, as follows.

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** If the Investor is an entity, it is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of organization, and it has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement, to make the Commitment and to perform its obligations under the Fund Documents. If the Investor is a natural person, he or she has full legal capacity to do so. The person signing for the Investor has been duly authorized, and this Agreement is a legal, valid and binding obligation of the Investor, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy and similar laws affecting creditors’ rights generally.

**5.2 No Conflict.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by the Investor do not violate its organizational documents, any law or regulation applicable to it, any order of any court or governmental body, or any agreement by which it or its assets are bound. No consent of any governmental body or other person is required for the Investor to make the Commitment, other than consents that have been obtained.

**5.3 Accredited Investor.** The Investor is an “accredited investor” as defined in Rule 501(a) of Regulation D under the Securities Act, and has indicated in Annex A the category under which it so qualifies. The Investor will notify the General Partner immediately if it ceases to be an accredited investor at any time before it is admitted to the Partnership. The Investor understands that the General Partner is relying on this representation to offer the Interest without registration under the Securities Act in reliance on Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act and Rule 506(b) of Regulation D.

**5.4 Qualified Purchaser.** The Investor is a Qualified Purchaser, and it has not been formed, reformed or recapitalized for the specific purpose of investing in the Partnership. If the Investor is a company that would be an investment company but for Section 3(c)(1) or Section 3(c)(7) of the Investment Company Act, it has obtained the consent of its beneficial owners that the Investment Company Act requires, and each of its beneficial owners is also a Qualified Purchaser to the extent that law applies. The Investor will give the General Partner the evidence of its status that the General Partner may reasonably request.

**5.5 Investment Intent.** The Investor is acquiring the Interest for its own account, for investment purposes and not with a view to resale or distribution, and not as a nominee or agent for any other person unless it has disclosed that fact in Annex A. No other person has a direct or indirect beneficial interest in the Interest except as disclosed in Annex A. The Investor has no present intention to sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of the Interest or any part of it.

**5.6 Sophistication and Credit Experience.** The Investor has such knowledge and experience in financial and business matters, including investment in private credit and in senior secured and unitranche loans, that it is capable of evaluating the merits and risks of an investment in the Partnership. The Investor is a Qualified Credit Investor, and it is able to bear the economic risk of the investment for the full term of the Partnership, including the possible loss of its entire investment, and it has no need for liquidity in the Interest.

**5.7 Access to Information; No Reliance.** The Investor has received and carefully reviewed the Fund Documents, has had the opportunity to ask questions of the Manager and to receive answers, and has obtained all information it considers necessary to make an informed decision. The Investor is not relying on any statement, projection or representation other than those in the Fund Documents. The Investor understands that any projection or target return in the Memorandum is a forward-looking estimate, that actual results may differ materially from it, and that no person has guaranteed a return of capital or any level of return.

**5.8 Restricted Securities.** The Investor understands that the Interest has not been registered under the Securities Act or the securities laws of any state, that the Interest is a “restricted security,” and that it cannot be resold unless it is registered or an exemption from registration is available. No market exists for the Interest and none is expected to develop. The Investor acknowledges that the transfer restrictions in Section 9 and in the Partnership Agreement apply to it for the entire term of the Partnership.

**5.9 Bad Actor Disqualification.** Neither the Investor nor any of its directors, executive officers, general partners, managing members or other officers who participate in the investment, and no beneficial owner of twenty percent or more of its voting securities, is subject to any of the disqualifying events described in Rule 506(d)(1) of Regulation D under the Securities Act, and the Investor will notify the General Partner promptly if that statement ceases to be accurate.

**5.10 Investment Company Status; Adviser.** The Investor understands that the Partnership will not be registered as an investment company under the Investment Company Act, that the Manager is not acting as the Investor’s fiduciary or adviser in connection with the Investor’s decision to subscribe, and that the Investor has made that decision independently, after consulting its own legal, tax and financial advisers to the extent it considered appropriate.

**5.11 Accuracy of Information.** All information the Investor has provided to the General Partner, the Manager and the Administrator, including in Annex A, is complete and correct in all material respects. The Investor agrees to provide further information, and to update that information promptly, as the General Partner or the Administrator may reasonably request in order to comply with applicable law or the requirements of the Subscription Facility.

# Section 6. Anti-Money Laundering, Sanctions and Know-Your-Customer

**6.1 Compliance with Anti-Money Laundering Laws.** The Investor represents that the funds it will use to make its contributions to the Partnership were not and will not be derived from, and will not be used to finance, any activity that violates Anti-Money Laundering Laws. The Investor has policies and procedures that are reasonably designed to prevent a violation of Anti-Money Laundering Laws, and it will provide the Administrator with any information the Administrator requests to verify the Investor’s identity and the origin of its funds.

**6.2 OFAC and Sanctions.** Neither the Investor nor any person that controls it, is controlled by it, owns a beneficial interest in it or acts on its behalf (a) is the subject or target of any Sanctions, (b) is named on any list of specially designated nationals or blocked persons maintained by OFAC, (c) is located, organized or resident in a country or territory that is the subject of comprehensive Sanctions, or (d) is a senior foreign political figure, a close family member or close associate of one, or an entity owned or controlled by one, unless it has disclosed that status in Annex A. The Investor will promptly notify the General Partner if any of these representations ceases to be true.

**6.3 Source of Funds.** Any Investor whose Commitment is more than $5,000,000 must give the Administrator written evidence of the source of the funds it will use to satisfy that Commitment, in a form acceptable to the Administrator, before the Closing at which the Investor is to be admitted. Acceptable evidence includes audited financial statements, a letter from the Investor’s auditor or bank, a plan funding certificate or an investment committee approval that identifies the source. The Administrator may ask any other Investor for the same evidence if it considers that necessary to meet its legal obligations.

**6.4 Beneficial Ownership.** The Investor will identify to the Administrator each person that directly or indirectly owns twenty-five percent or more of its equity or voting power, and each person that exercises control over it, and will provide a government-issued identity document for each such person together with proof of residential address. An Investor that is a plan, an insurance company or a foundation will identify its trustees, officers or other control persons, and the Administrator may accept alternative documentation that Anti-Money Laundering Laws allow.

**6.5 Non-U.S. Investors.** An Investor that is organized or resident outside the United States will, in addition, provide certified copies of its formation documents or identity documents, translated into English where the Administrator requests, and a letter from a regulated financial institution that confirms the Investor’s account relationship and the origin of its funds. The Investor will notify the Administrator promptly of any change in its country of residence.

**6.6 Reports and Disclosure.** The Investor understands that the General Partner, the Manager and the Administrator may be required to disclose information about the Investor and its investment to governmental authorities, to refuse or freeze a transaction, and to suspend or reject a subscription or withdrawal, in each case without notice to the Investor and without liability, where that is necessary to comply with Anti-Money Laundering Laws or Sanctions.

**6.7 Ongoing Obligations.** The representations in this Section 6 are continuing. The Investor will notify the General Partner if there is a material change in its ownership, control or source of funds, and agrees that it will not transfer any part of its Interest to a person that has not completed the Administrator’s onboarding procedures.

# Section 7. Tax Matters

**7.1 Tax Forms.** The Investor will deliver to the Administrator, with this Agreement, a valid and completed Internal Revenue Service tax form that establishes its status. An Investor that is a United States person will deliver IRS Form W-9. An Investor that is a non-United States individual will deliver IRS Form W-8BEN, and a non-United States entity will deliver the form that applies to it, including IRS Form W-8BEN-E or IRS Form W-8IMY. The Investor will deliver a replacement form promptly if any information on its form becomes inaccurate, and in any event before the form expires.

**7.2 FATCA.** The Investor will provide the information, forms and certifications that the Partnership reasonably requests to comply with FATCA, including its global intermediary identification number if it has one, and acknowledges that the Partnership may disclose that information to the Internal Revenue Service and to any other taxing authority. If the Investor fails to provide that information, the Partnership may withhold tax from distributions to the Investor at the rate that FATCA requires, and may treat any amount withheld as having been distributed to the Investor.

**7.3 Common Reporting Standard.** The Investor acknowledges that the Partnership is, or may become, subject to the OECD Common Reporting Standard (“CRS”) and that the Investor may be required to provide self-certifications of its tax residence, its taxpayer identification number in each jurisdiction of residence, and, for an entity, information about its controlling persons. The Investor agrees to supply those self-certifications and agrees that the Partnership may report information to the Internal Revenue Service for onward exchange with the tax authority of the Investor’s country of residence under CRS.

**7.4 Withholding.** The Partnership may withhold and pay over to the relevant taxing authority any amount that the Code or other applicable law requires it to withhold from a payment to the Investor. Any amount withheld will be treated as having been distributed to the Investor, and the Investor will indemnify the Partnership for any withholding liability that exceeds the amount it has actually withheld from the Investor, to the extent attributable to the Investor.

**7.5 Tax-Exempt Investors and UBTI.** An Investor that is exempt from United States federal income tax acknowledges that the Partnership may borrow money, including under the Subscription Facility, and may hold investments that generate income that is “unrelated business taxable income” (“UBTI”) or that is treated as debt-financed income under the Code. The General Partner will use reasonable efforts to limit the generation of UBTI, but gives no assurance that no UBTI will be allocated to the Investor, and the Investor has relied on its own tax advisers in deciding whether to invest.

**7.6 Effectively Connected Income.** A non-United States Investor acknowledges that the Partnership may realize income that is effectively connected with a United States trade or business, in particular from loan origination or from the receipt of fees for services in connection with loans, and that the Investor may as a result be required to file United States tax returns and to bear withholding on that income. The Partnership may use a blocker entity for that purpose and may require the Investor to hold its Interest through one.

**7.7 Partnership Representative.** The Investor agrees that the person designated under the Partnership Agreement as the “partnership representative” will have sole authority to act for the Partnership in any tax audit, and that the Investor will be bound by the actions of that person. The Investor will provide the information that the partnership representative requires to make any election on behalf of the Partnership, and to determine any imputed underpayment.

# Section 8. ERISA and Benefit Plan Matters

**8.1 Benefit Plan Investor Status.** The Investor has indicated in Question 6 of Annex A whether it is a Benefit Plan Investor, and the answer it has given is correct. The Investor understands that the General Partner intends to conduct the Partnership so that the assets of the Partnership are not treated as Plan Assets under ERISA, and that the General Partner will monitor participation by Benefit Plan Investors in accordance with the 25% limitation described in Section 8.4. The Investor will notify the General Partner immediately if its status as a Benefit Plan Investor, or the percentage of its assets that constitute Plan Assets, changes.

**8.2 Annex B Required.** Annex B (ERISA Representations) must be completed by each Investor that is an ERISA plan, but is not required for plans subject to Section 4975 of the Code (e.g., IRAs). An Investor that is an entity whose underlying assets include Plan Assets must also complete Annex B. The General Partner may ask any other Investor to complete Annex B if it considers that necessary to administer the 25% test in Section 8.3, and the Investor will do so within five Business Days of the request.

**8.3 The 25% Test.** The General Partner will use reasonable efforts to ensure that, at all times, Benefit Plan Investors hold less than twenty-five percent of the total value of each class of equity interests in the Partnership, disregarding for that purpose any Interest held by the General Partner, the Manager or any of their affiliates, or by any other person who has discretionary authority or control over the assets of the Partnership or who provides investment advice for a fee, direct or indirect, with respect to those assets (and any affiliate of such a person). If the total participation by Benefit Plan Investors equals or exceeds that limit, the Partnership will hold Plan Assets and the Significant Participation test of Section 3(42) of ERISA will be met. The Investor acknowledges that the General Partner may take the steps in Section 8.8 to avoid that result.

**8.4 Insurance Company General Accounts.** An Investor that is an insurance company and that is using assets of its general account to fund the Commitment represents that: (a) it has determined, and will notify the General Partner of, the maximum percentage of the assets of its general account that constitute Plan Assets, as shown in Annex B; (b) it will not exceed that percentage without the consent of the General Partner; (c) to the extent it constitutes Plan Assets, the assets of its general account are held in accordance with Section 401(c) of ERISA and the regulations under it; and (d) it has provided to the General Partner the most recent calculation of that percentage, as of a date no more than ninety days before the date of this Agreement. For purposes of the 25% test, the General Partner will treat the Investor as a Benefit Plan Investor only to the extent of that percentage.

**8.5 Fiduciary Matters.** An Investor that is a Benefit Plan Investor represents that the person who has made the decision to invest on its behalf is a fiduciary who is independent of the General Partner, the Manager and their affiliates, is capable of evaluating investment risks independently, has exercised independent judgment in evaluating the investment, and is responsible for exercising independent judgment. The Investor understands that none of the General Partner, the Manager or their affiliates has provided investment advice, or has been relied on for investment advice, for a fee or other compensation within the meaning of Section 3(21) of ERISA, in connection with the Investor’s decision to subscribe.

**8.6 Prohibited Transactions.** An Investor that is a Benefit Plan Investor represents that its investment in the Partnership will not constitute or result in a non-exempt prohibited transaction under Section 406 of ERISA or Section 4975 of the Code, and will not cause the Partnership, the General Partner or the Manager to be a fiduciary of the Investor within the meaning of ERISA. The Investor will promptly notify the General Partner if it learns that the representations in this Section 8.6 have ceased to be true.

**8.7 Governmental, Church and Non-U.S. Plans.** An Investor that is a governmental plan, a church plan or a non-United States plan, and that is not subject to Title I of ERISA or Section 4975 of the Code, represents that it is also not subject to any federal, state, local or non-United States law that is substantially similar to those provisions and that could cause the underlying assets of the Partnership to be treated as assets of the Investor, or, if it is subject to such a law, that its investment in the Partnership will not violate that law.

**8.8 Corrective Measures.** If the General Partner determines that the Partnership would otherwise hold Plan Assets, it may, without the consent of any investor, take any of the following steps to the extent necessary to restore compliance with the 25% test: (a) reject or reduce the subscription of any Benefit Plan Investor; (b) require any Benefit Plan Investor to withdraw from the Partnership, in whole or in part, on not less than ten Business Days’ notice, and to receive the value of the withdrawn Interest determined by the General Partner in good faith; (c) refuse to consent to a transfer of an Interest to a Benefit Plan Investor; and (d) take any other action the Partnership Agreement allows. The Investor agrees to cooperate with any step taken under this Section 8.8.

**8.9 ERISA Indemnity.** Each Investor that makes a representation under this Section 8 or in Annex B agrees to indemnify the Partnership, the General Partner, the Manager and their respective affiliates against any loss, liability or expense, including reasonable fees of counsel, that arises from the inaccuracy of that representation, including any excise tax or penalty that is imposed because the Partnership holds Plan Assets.
