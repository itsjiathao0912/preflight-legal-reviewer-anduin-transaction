Subscriptions for Shares up to the minimum offering amount as set forth in the Offering Circular may only be made through the Clearing Agent. Subsequent subscriptions after the minimum offering amount has been reached may be made through the Clearing Agent via its online portal or through the Escrow Agent by wire or check and submission of this written Subscription Agreement.
