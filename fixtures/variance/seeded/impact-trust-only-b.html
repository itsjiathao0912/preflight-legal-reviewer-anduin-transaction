3. Subscription

The minimum initial subscription amount for Class A is US$ 250,000.

The general partner may accept a smaller amount for existing holders.

Subscriptions received after December 31, 2024 are held to the next closing.

Each holder of the Units receives a quarterly report.

A transfer of the Units needs written consent.

See Section 5 for the payment terms.

4. Representations

5. Payment

Subscription monies are paid by wire transfer.

Notices are sent by e-mail to the address in the register.

Reports are published on the Internet page of the administrator.

A copy goes by e-mail to the custodian.

Payments on the Units are made in US dollars.
