The problem

Errors in the paper carry into the digital flow.

  • A cross-reference points to a section that is not there.
  • A conditional skip jumps to the wrong place, or a section no investor can reach.
  • Every feeder, parallel and prior version has to be read again.

Doing this by hand over forty pages is slow and uneven.

The user

The Digitization Service Analyst.

ReadsSub docs in HTML, PDF, DOCX or scans.
DecidesWhat is a real defect, what is house style.
AsksCounsel to confirm anything that changes the form.
Approach

Deterministic first, language model second.

Rules decideA logic graph and 13 detectors check references, skips, flow, terms and investor paths. Every finding cites its text.
Models judgeFree models read only graph-selected passages for ambiguity, contradiction and typos. Output is checked; unlocatable quotes are dropped.

The app works with AI switched off.

Triage

Keyboard-first, with a reason for every dismissal.

  • Accept, dismiss (reason required, undo available) or send to counsel.
  • Provenance on every row: rule id, or model plus fresh or cache.
  • A remembered decision never hides a blocking finding.
Compare versions

See drift between related documents.

  • Sections are aligned across feeder, parallel and prior versions.
  • Changes are classed, with a short narrative of what moved.
  • Not yet: scanned documents, and diff-only rows as counsel questions.
Counsel query sheet and gate

Confirmed issues become questions. Critical ones gate the build.

  • Export the sheet as DOCX; record answers in the app.
  • The gate reads Blocked - N until every blocking question is answered, then Clear.
Firm memory

The tool remembers how a firm decides.

  • Three matching dismissals propose a rule for that firm.
  • Approved rules feed the AI prompt; every change is in an audit trail.
  • Reached from the sidebar nav.

Parse in the browser; the Worker only proxies.

Cost, limits and privacy

Free models, a hard cap, and documents that stay local.

  • Free OpenRouter models only; paid path disabled; circuit breaker on any billed use.
  • Per visitor per day: 3 fresh-AI documents, 8 AI requests, 6 OCR pages.
  • Only clause passages (or page images with AI on) leave the browser, after a disclosure.
  • Snippets and hashes only in the database; sandbox data purged after 30 days.
Evidence

Tested at every layer.

  • Core 2,914 tests, worker 525, web unit 196 at the last recorded closeout.
  • Golden corpus on real SEC documents, seeded-defect fixtures, property tests.
  • Browser tests, an accessibility scan, and a live smoke run against the deployed site.
Known misses

What it does not do yet.

  • Structurer misses on a few samples; some statutory citations and sub-letter references (4B, 3A) are not resolved.
  • No missing-target cross-reference detector; no edit of finding text or severity.
  • No compare for scanned documents. Live AI quality is only lightly measured.
  • Not production: no accounts, no CI, sandbox tenancy only.
